# Service mesh: Istio ambient, Linkerd, mTLS

This lesson follows the topic synthesis `synth/LNX-312/LNX-312-T09.md`, which carries the sources, the history and the failure cases.

## What you will be able to do

- Explain how a workload gets an identity and how mutual TLS uses it.
- Compare sidecar, Rust micro-proxy and ambient designs on cost and features.
- Write a strict mTLS and an identity-based authorisation policy, and plan a safe rollout.

## Identity and mTLS

The control plane signs short-lived certificates after validating the Pod's service-account token. The certificate carries a SPIFFE-style name: trust domain, namespace and service account. Both sides of a connection present one, so traffic is encrypted and the caller is known. Roots are supplied by the organisation (offline root, intermediate in the mesh); an expired intermediate stops all new connections.

## Three designs

| Design | Data plane | Strength | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sidecar (Istio) | Envoy per Pod | full L7 everywhere | memory per Pod, injection, lifecycle bugs |
| Linkerd | Rust proxy per Pod | small, mTLS by default | fewer extension points; since 2.15 the project publishes only edge releases and stable releases come from vendor distributions such as Buoyant Enterprise for Linkerd |
| Ambient (Istio) | per-node ztunnel (L4, HBONE) plus optional waypoints | cheap mTLS, no injection | waypoint design, CNI coexistence, feature gaps (multicluster is Beta and limited to multi-network setups as of Istio 1.29) |

## Policy

`PeerAuthentication` sets PERMISSIVE or STRICT. `AuthorizationPolicy` names allowed principals and ports (L4) or methods and paths (L7, needs a sidecar or waypoint). Identities come from certificates, so a spoofed source address does not help an attacker. Keep network policy underneath as an independent layer.

## Worked example: strict broke the probes

After switching a namespace to STRICT, Pods flapped. Kubelet probes carry no workload certificate. Fix: let the injector rewrite probes or exempt the probe port, and test a Job and a start-up dependency before the next namespace.
