# Milestone 5: profiling and flame graphs

This milestone follows the topic synthesis `synth/LNX-403/LNX-403-T05.md`. Versions and defaults change; confirm them in current project documents (to verify).

## Goal

Find where the service spends its time under a repeatable load and prove one improvement.

## Lessons this milestone relies on

- LNX-220-T11 (profiling with perf and flame graphs)
- LNX-307-T09 (reliable benchmarking)
- LNX-307-T05 (perf and eBPF tools)
- LNX-320-T13 (NUMA and cache-effect measurement)

## Steps

1. Fix the machine state: governor, frequency, quiet system, pinned load generator.
2. Measure the baseline at least three times and record the spread.
3. Record stack samples for at least thirty seconds and draw a flame graph with resolved stacks.
4. Pick the widest avoidable box, make one change, and measure again the same way.
5. Write the result, including anything that did not help.

## Acceptance

- The evidence file passes the check.
- The flame graph and commands are in your repository.
- The gain is larger than the run-to-run spread.
