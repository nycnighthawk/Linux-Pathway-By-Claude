# Milestone 6: post-incident review

This milestone follows the topic synthesis `synth/LNX-401/LNX-401-T06.md`. Product versions and defaults change; confirm them in current vendor documents (to verify).

## Goal

Write a blameless review of a real incident from your month.

## Lessons this milestone relies on

- LNX-307-T01 and T10 (runbooks, on-call)
- LNX-307-T06 (kdump and crash analysis, if relevant)
- LNX-303-T10 (forensics basics, if relevant)

## Steps

1. Pick an incident from your log (or one you injected).
2. Reconstruct the timeline from logs, alerts and commits, in one time zone.
3. Compute time to detect and time to recover from the timeline.
4. Write at least two causes that name conditions in the system.
5. Write actions with an owner, a due date, a type and a result you can verify.

## Acceptance

- The grader passes on your review text.
- Every cause has an action and every action has an owner.
- A colleague who was not there can follow it.

## Lab

[Grader lab](lab:incident-review) checks the mechanical parts of this milestone. Passing it is necessary but not enough: the evidence in your repository is what is marked. See the brief for the rubric.
