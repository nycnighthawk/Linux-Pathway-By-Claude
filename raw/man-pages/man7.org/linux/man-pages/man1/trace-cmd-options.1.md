## [](#NAME)NAME

           trace-cmd-options - list available options from trace-cmd plugins

## [](#SYNOPSIS)SYNOPSIS

           trace-cmd options

## [](#DESCRIPTION)DESCRIPTION

           The trace-cmd(1) options command will examine all the trace-cmd
           plugins that are used by trace-cmd report(1) and list them.

## [](#SEE_ALSO)SEE ALSO

           trace-cmd(1), trace-cmd-record(1), trace-cmd-start(1),
           trace-cmd-stop(1), trace-cmd-extract(1), trace-cmd-reset(1),
