## [](#NAME)NAME

           pdfman - render a manual page in PDF

## [](#SYNOPSIS)SYNOPSIS

           pdfman [man-options] [section] page

## [](#DESCRIPTION)DESCRIPTION

           The pdfman command renders a manual page in PDF.  All the
           arguments are interpreted by man(1).

## [](#SEE_ALSO)SEE ALSO

           man(1), groff(1), gropdf(1), xdg-open(1)
